I am a lecturer at Beaconhouse International College and an MS graduate. I teach Artificial Intelligence, Databases and Data Science, and I build and train AI models in my own research.

My research interests sit where people, data and the planet meet. I am interested in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and how people experience technology, in deep learning for real-world problems, and in remote sensing — using satellite and radar (SAR) imagery for climate and disaster response.

I believe the best way to understand a model is to build it, break it, and then explain it to a classroom. My motto: teaching what I learn, learning what I teach.

You can find my code, notebooks and experiments on GitHub, and my problem-solving practice on LeetCode.
